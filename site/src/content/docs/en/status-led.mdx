ShutterBridge shows what it's doing through the on-board LED at a glance - what
you see depends on your board.

## {BOARDS["esp32s3-zero"].name}

This board has an RGB LED, so states are told apart by colour:

<LedStatus variant="rgb" />

The LED reflects the camera link first: once a camera is connected it shows green
(or blinking red while recording), regardless of Wi-Fi. Blue only appears when no
camera is connected but someone is on the config Wi-Fi. Violet overrides
everything while [Betaflight bridge](/docs/betaflight-bridge) mode is active.
