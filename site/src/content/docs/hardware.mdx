ShutterBridge targets the **{BOARD_NAME}** (ESP32-S3FH4R2: 4 MB flash, 2 MB PSRAM,
native USB). Other ESP32-S3 boards may work but are untested.

<img src="/docs/hardware/esp32-s3.webp" style={{ height: "150px" }} alt="Waveshare ESP32 S3 Mini Board" />

## You'll need

- The {BOARD_NAME} board.
- A flight controller running **Betaflight 2025.12 or newer** with a
  free UART.
- A supported action camera - a DJI Osmo / Action, or a GoPro HERO9 or newer.
- A data-capable USB-C cable for flashing.
- A soldering iron, basic soldering skills

See the [compatibility chart](/docs/compatibility) for the full camera compatibility matrix.
