Connect to the `ShutterBridge` Wi-Fi access point and open `http://10.0.0.1`.

The interface has four tabs - **Camera**, **Channels**, **OSD** and **System** -
a version pill in the header, and a **Save** button pinned to the bottom. The UI
adapts to the camera you've bound: controls a given camera can't do are hidden.

<Admonition type="note" title="Saving and reboots">
  Save writes everything to the board at once. Most settings apply immediately.
  Changing the **camera**, **type**, or **Wi-Fi name/password** reboots the
  board to apply - the UI does this for you and reconnects on its own.\
  See [first-time setup](/docs/setup).
</Admonition>

## Camera

Live status and the camera link.

<ThemedImage
    alt="Camera tab"
    lightSrc="/docs/webui/light/camera.webp"
    darkSrc="/docs/webui/dark/camera.webp"
    maxWidth="600px" />

### Live status

When a camera is online, this card mirrors what it's doing:

- **REC** indicator - lit while recording.
- **Elapsed** - current recording time (mm:ss).
- **Mode** - Photo or Video.
- **Format** - resolution and frame rate, e.g. `4K 60fps` (not reported by the Osmo Nano).
- **Battery** - percentage, with a bar.
- **SD free** - remaining card space.
- **Time left** - estimated recording time left on the card (mm:ss).

### Connection

Shows the bound camera's model and MAC (or **No camera / not bound**) and a status
pill:

- **connecting** - reaching the camera.
- **Accept PIN on camera** - approve the pairing PIN on a DJI camera's screen.
- **Camera online** - connected and reporting.
- **Camera offline** - not connected.

### Binding

**Bind new camera** opens the pairing wizard: pick the **Camera type** (DJI Osmo
(Nano) / DJI Action / 360 / GoPro), **Scan**, then **Select camera** from the list.
The board saves the choice and reboots to connect. Binding is a one-time step;
after that it auto-reconnects on every boot.

### Controls

Manual controls for the bound camera. This card only appears for cameras that
support a mode switch, and it's disabled while **Record on arm** is active (in that
mode the camera is driven automatically).

- **Mode** - switch the camera between **Video** and **Photo** (Action / 360 and
  GoPro; the Osmo Nano can't switch over BLE).
- **Preset group** (GoPro only) - load the **Video**, **Photo** or **Timelapse**
  preset group.
- **Preset by ID** (GoPro only) - load a specific preset by its hex ID.

## Channels

Map RC switches to camera actions.

<ThemedImage
    alt="Channels tab"
    lightSrc="/docs/webui/light/channels.webp"
    darkSrc="/docs/webui/dark/channels.webp"
    maxWidth="550px" />

### Recording

- **Mode**
  * **Record on arm (default)**: starts recording when you arm and stops when you disarm
  * **Manual**: drives the camera entirely from the channel functions
  below
  (manual controls are turned off in this mode)
- **Record start delay** / **Record stop delay** - milliseconds to wait before
  starting/stopping, so you don't clip the beginning or end of a clip

### Channel functions

Each function has an **AUX channel** selector and a **range** slider (in
microseconds). The function is active while that channel sits inside the range; a
green marker shows the channel's live value.\
Setting a function's channel to **Off** disables it.

Functions available (the UI hides ones your camera can't do):

- **Shutter** - the main trigger. In Photo mode it takes a photo; in Video mode it
  records. Its **Video trigger** option picks the behaviour: **Momentary** (each
  flick toggles recording) or **2-position** (records while the switch is held).
- **Camera Mode** - switch the camera between photo and video from a channel
  (Action / 360 and GoPro). **Momentary** toggles each flick; **2-position** sets
  Photo in-range and Video out.
- **Preset Video / Preset Photo / Preset Timelapse** (GoPro only) - load a preset
  group from a channel.

## OSD

Choose what telemetry the four OSD slots show.

<ThemedImage
    alt="OSD tab"
    lightSrc="/docs/webui/light/osd.webp"
    darkSrc="/docs/webui/dark/osd.webp"
    maxWidth="500px" />

Each **Custom Msg** slot (0-3) maps to a field: **Off**, **Rec status**,
**Battery %**, **Mode**, **SD free**, **Time left**, **Resolution** or **FPS**.
Resolution and FPS aren't available on the Osmo Nano.

Assign a field here, then enable and position the matching **Custom Message**
elements in Betaflight's OSD tab - see [FC Wiring & Betaflight](/docs/wiring).

## System

Device-wide settings.

<ThemedImage
    alt="System tab"
    lightSrc="/docs/webui/light/system.webp"
    darkSrc="/docs/webui/dark/system.webp"
    maxWidth="500px" />

### Wi-Fi

- **Name** / **Password** - the config access point's credentials (changing these
  reboots the board).
- **Off when armed** - drop the config Wi-Fi while the FC reports armed, so the
  radio is dedicated to the camera link in flight.

### Bluetooth

- **TX power** - **Low**, **Medium** or **High**. Higher reaches further but can
  interfere more with a nearby RC receiver. Lower it if your control-link range
  drops with the bridge powered on.

### Clock (GoPro only)

- **Sync from GPS** - set the GoPro's clock from the flight controller's GPS time.
  Needs a GPS fix on the FC.
- **UTC offset** - hours to shift from UTC for local time (quarter-hour steps).

### Device

- **Reboot** - restart the board.
