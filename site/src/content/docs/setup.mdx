With the firmware flashed and the board wired to your FC, the rest is done from
the on-device web interface over Wi-Fi. No app to install.

## 1. Connect to the config Wi-Fi

Power the board (USB or off the FC). It hosts a Wi-Fi access point for setup:

- **Network:** `ShutterBridge`
- **Password:** `shutterbridge`

Connect to it from a phone or laptop, then open **`http://10.0.0.1`** in a
browser. You'll land on the **Camera** tab.

<Admonition type="tip">
  If your phone drops the connection because there's no internet, choose "stay
  connected" when prompted. The board's LED turns solid blue while a device is
  connected to the config Wi-Fi.
</Admonition>

## 2. Pair your camera

On the **Camera** tab, run the connection wizard:

1. **Camera type** - pick your backend: `Osmo (Nano)`, `Action / 360` or `GoPro`.
2. **Scan** - the board scans for nearby cameras over Bluetooth. Make sure the
   camera is on and its Bluetooth is enabled.
3. **Select camera** - choose yours from the list. The board saves it and will
   auto-reconnect on every future boot, so this is a one-time step.

### Accept the pairing

The **Connection** pill at the top shows where you are:

- **connecting** - reaching the camera.
- **Accept PIN on camera** - a DJI camera is asking you to confirm pairing.
  Approve the PIN prompt on the camera's own screen.
- **Camera online** - paired and ready.
- **Camera offline** - not connected (out of range, off, or not yet paired).

<Admonition type="note">
  DJI Action series cameras confirm pairing with a PIN on the camera itself; GoPro bonds on the
  camera the first time. After that first pairing, reconnection is automatic.
</Admonition>

Once the camera is online, its live status (record state, battery, SD, mode)
appears on the Camera tab, and the board's LED goes solid green.

## 3. Map your controls

Switch to the **Channels** tab to decide how the camera is triggered:

- **Mode** - `Record on arm` starts/stops recording with your quad's arm switch,
  or `Manual` to drive it entirely from a switch.
- **Record start / stop delay** - small delays so you don't clip the start or end
  of a shot.
- **Channel mapping** - assign an AUX channel and range to actions like shutter,
  photo/video mode and preset switching.

See the [Web UI reference](/docs/web-ui) for every control on each tab.

## 4. Set up the OSD

The camera status is drawn into Betaflight's Custom Message OSD slots. Assign a
field (record status, battery, mode, SD free, time left, resolution, FPS) to each
slot on the **OSD** tab, make sure to enable and position those elements in Betaflight -
see [FC Wiring & Betaflight](/docs/wiring).

## 5. System options

On the **System** tab you can set the Wi-Fi name/password, turn Wi-Fi off when
armed, choose the Bluetooth TX power, and (GoPro only) sync the camera clock from
your FC's GPS time.

## You're set

<Admonition type="danger" title="Wake the camera before every flight">
  On the DJI Osmo Nano, if recording starts while the camera is asleep (standby /
  screen off), the clip still records but comes out corrupted - stuck at a very
  low frame rate. This is a current limitation with no known fix.

  Wake the camera and let it finish booting before arming.
  Good practice on any camera; on the Osmo Nano it's essential.
</Admonition>

Arm on the bench (props off) and confirm the OSD reflects the camera. If anything
misbehaves, the [Status LED](/docs/status-led) tells you what state the board is
in, and [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) covers the common snags.
