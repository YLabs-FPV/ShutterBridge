ShutterBridge is firmware for an ESP32-S3 board. It connects over Bluetooth LE
to a DJI or GoPro action camera and talks to a Betaflight flight controller
over MSP. The camera's status is drawn on your OSD, and RC switch positions
are mapped to camera actions like record, photo and mode switch.


## How it works

- **Camera link** - Bluetooth LE to the action camera (DJI DUML/R-SDK or Open GoPro).
- **FC link** - MSP over a spare UART to Betaflight.
- **OSD** - record state, battery, SD and timers are pushed onto the goggles feed.
- **Actions** - RC switches trigger record, photo and photo/video mode.
