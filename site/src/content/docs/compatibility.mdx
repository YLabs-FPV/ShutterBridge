<Admonition type="note">
  The DJI Osmo Nano can't switch photo/video over BLE, so the shutter only takes a
  photo when the camera is already in Photo mode.
</Admonition>

<Admonition type="warning" title="Wake the Osmo Nano before recording">
  On the Osmo Nano, starting a recording while the camera is asleep (standby /
  screen off) produces a corrupted, very-low-FPS clip. Wake it and wait until the
  LED on the front is green before you arm. A current limitation with no known fix.
</Admonition>

## Tested cameras

The matrix above shows what each _backend_ supports. Below is which specific
camera models have actually been run on hardware - by the maintainer and by the
community.

| Camera             | Backend          | Result                | Tested by  |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | --------------------- | ---------- |
| DJI Osmo Nano      | Osmo (Nano)      | Works (limitations\*) | Maintainer |
| DJI Osmo Action 4  | Action / 360     | Works fully           | Maintainer |
| GoPro (HERO9+)     | GoPro            | Not yet tested        | -          |

_\* See the recording limitation noted above._

<Admonition type="tip" title="Tried it with your camera? Let us know">
  Reports build this table - even "it just works" is useful. Share your result
  with the <a href={`${GITHUB_URL}/issues/new?template=camera_report.yml`}>camera
  compatibility report</a>. Include the model, the backend you selected, the
  firmware version, and anything that didn't work.
</Admonition>
